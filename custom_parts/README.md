# SpotMicro → Jetson Orin Nano external-mount conversion

Generated by `make_jetson_parts.py`. Re-run that script after editing any parameter.

## Why these parts exist

The stock SpotMicro body was built around an Arduino Mega. Its internal deck
(`plate.stl`) is 107 × 95 mm and the bay is shared with the battery, PCA9685,
two buck converters and the 12-servo harness — an Orin Nano Dev Kit
(103 × 90.5 × 34.7 mm with heatsink, 7–25 W) does not fit and would cook itself
inside a 2.25 mm PLA shell. So the Jetson moves **on top of the cover**, in open
air, and the internal bay keeps only the battery and power/servo electronics.

**`plate.stl` from the original kit is now unused — do not print it.**

## Interface (all measured off the original cover, not assumed)

- Crown flat face: **Y = 124**, X 30…210, Z 17.2…96.8
- The cover's 4 existing screw holes (**Ø5.17 at X 35/205 × Z 37/77**) are
  reused and unchanged — the deck bolts through them into the shoulder blocks,
  so deck + cover lift off as one unit for access to the bay.
- Verified clearances: deck↔cover interference **0.000 cm³**;
  heatsink↔deck interference **0.000 cm³**.
- Stack height above the crown: **44.7 mm**.

## What changed on the top cover

Nothing structural — the shell, the crown and all 4 original screw holes are untouched.
Added through-slots in the crown only:

- 2 × CSI ribbon slots (26 × 4 mm) at X = 48 and X = 192 — one at each end, so it
  works whichever end you bolt `F_cover` to. The unused one is just another vent.
- 1 × main harness slot (40 × 12 mm) at centre, for the 5 V feed and the I²C pair.
- 4 × bay vents (60 × 4 mm). These sit directly under the deck's central cutout,
  so the Orin's downdraft fan pushes air into the body. Add exit vents to
  `non-mega_B_cover_mg.stl` if the bay runs warm.

## Deck features

- 4 mm plate, side rails for stiffness, central 72 × 54 mm cutout for airflow + cables.
- 4 × Ø7 mm standoff posts, 6 mm tall, with 2.1 mm pilot holes for M2.5 self-tapping
  screws. The 6 mm lift is deliberate — it gives the board underside airflow.
- 4 × L-shaped corner guides sized to the 100 × 79 mm carrier PCB. Their top is
  flush with the PCB top face so the wider 103 × 90.5 mm heatsink clears them.
- 4 × strap slots for a velcro/zip-tie restraint.
- 2 × M3 hole grids (3 × 7 on a 12 mm pitch) on the end strips for the DHT22,
  MQ-135, NEO-6M and PMS5003.

## ⚠ Verify before printing the deck

`HOLE_SPAN_X = 86.0` and `HOLE_SPAN_Z = 58.0` in the script are an **estimate** of
the carrier board's M2.5 mounting-hole spacing. Put calipers on your board, edit
those two numbers, re-run the script. Nothing else changes.

Print the deck **last**, once the board is in hand. Until then the corner guides
plus a strap already hold the board, so a wrong spacing is not fatal.

## Print settings

Both parts print **support-free** — every deck feature rises from a flat bottom face,
and the cover prints crown-down exactly like the original.

PLA+, 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 40 % gyroid. The deck is a structural plate carrying
~300 g at height — do not drop below 3 walls.

Filament: deck ≈ 45 g, cover ≈ 33 g (the cover it replaces was ≈ 35 g).

If the bay ever runs hot, reprint the cover in PETG — PLA softens around 60 °C.
