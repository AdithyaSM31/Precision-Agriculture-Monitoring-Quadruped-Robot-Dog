AgriBot Sensor Wiring

How the SGP30, MCP9808, PPD42 and two HC-SR04 sensors connect to the XIAO ESP32-S3 on its expansion board. Every pin here matches agri_monitor/firmware/sensor_node/sensor_node.ino.

Schematic

Wire colours show function. Every 5 V sensor output reaches the XIAO through a divider.

Breadboard layout

Three dividers, nine 1 kΩ resistors (brown, black, red, gold). Each shaded column is one connected strip of five holes.

Before you power on

Never plug the 3S LiPo into the expansion board

Its battery socket takes one 3.7 V cell. 11.1 V destroys the XIAO immediately.

Take the XIAO wire from the tap

The tap is the column between R1 and R2. A wire taken from the sensor's side of R1 sends the full 5 V into the pin.

Wire with USB unplugged, then check for shorts

Before the first power-up, use a multimeter on continuity between the + and − rails. It must not beep.

Leave the microSD slot empty

D8 and D9 are its data lines. A card in the slot fights the downward ultrasonic sensor.

Mount the PPD42 upright, connector at the bottom

Its heater moves air through the sensor by convection, which only works in this orientation.

How the camera fits in

The ESP32-CAM needs no wiring beyond its MB board and a USB cable. It reaches the laptop over Wi-Fi.
